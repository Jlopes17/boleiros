# Boleiros product direction

Boleiros combines club management inspired by classic Brasfoot with a playable footballer career inspired by New Star Soccer 5.

## Priority order

1. Correct season and competition logic
2. Complete, versioned club and player database
3. Transfers, contracts, injuries, suspensions and squad registration
4. Promotion, relegation and season rollover
5. Coach match center with meaningful tactical decisions
6. Complete mode with responsive player control and useful teammate AI
7. Player life, relationships, money, training and career decisions
8. Mobile UX, accessibility, performance and save reliability

## Required realism

- The user can only play the next scheduled fixture.
- A match mode is selected before kickoff and cannot change during the match.
- Weekly actions cannot be repeated without advancing time.
- Competitions use their own format rather than one generic league template.
- AI clubs play their fixtures, manage fatigue and change squads.
- Player development depends on age, minutes, form, training and potential.
- Finances use attendance, wages, prizes, sponsorships and transfer payments.
